SatooriLabs

Interactive learning platform for teacher-created simulations. A safe environment where students make decisions, explore consequences, and learn through experience.

What is SatooriLabs?

SatooriLabs allows teachers to build their own simulations according to the level, subject, and needs of their students. Instead of using rigid, pre-built scenarios, teachers can design interactive learning journeys that support real decision-making, reflection, and engagement.

Student Area

Students can access simulations, complete activities, and review their own progress through the Student Library.

Teacher Area

Teachers can design simulations, manage resources, review progress, generate QR codes, and access tracking tools.

Pilot Access

Use the pilot version to test the platform in class and explore the current simulations with students.
