QR Code Generator

Paste the link to a simulation (teacher custom or built-in). Then generate a QR code for students.

Tip: The QR is just the URL. Example links: • pilot.html • health_simulation_v2.html • any custom file like teacher_simulation_001.html

What students do

1) Open camera / QR scanner 2) Scan QR 3) Simulation opens instantly Works on phone, tablet, laptop.
